For Smiley ♥

I built this because a text message felt too small, and because you deserve something someone had to sit down and actually make.

You have this way of making an ordinary day feel like it was worth showing up for. Nothing dramatic — just you walking in, saying something ridiculous, and suddenly the whole day lands differently. That is a rare thing, and I notice it more often than I say it.

I love that you smile before you speak. I love that you take people seriously. I love the version of me that exists around you: calmer, funnier, less in a hurry.

I’m not going to promise you perfect. I’ll promise you present — that I’ll keep choosing you on the loud days and the quiet ones, and that I intend to be around for the long, unremarkable, wonderful middle of it all.

Yours, always — Peter
